# Parent And Teacher Readability

The report is mainly for parents and may also be read by teachers.

## Required Reader Experience

The adult reader should feel:

- “This sounds like my child.”
- “I understand what the result means.”
- “I know what I can do next.”
- “The report respects me and does not blame the child.”

## Structure Checks

Expected section titles or clear equivalents:

- 前言
- 核心成长画像
- 详细指标分析
- 成长支持建议
- 报告说明 / 边界说明

If the report uses “评价” as a main section title, suggest changing it to “分析”.

## Style Checks

Flag:

- excessive jargon: “收敛分析”“认知穿透力”“可视化的时间知觉重塑”等
- internet slang: “脑洞大开”等
- inflated wording: “极其”“极度”“完美”“顶尖”“一流”“无懈可击”
- mechanical AI transitions: “首先、其次、最后、综上所述” used heavily
- chatty opening: “好的，下面是……”

## Suggestion Checks

成长支持建议 should include:

- an overall feedback paragraph
- detailed advice linked to data
- scene, action, adult wording, and first step
- domains such as learning, thinking, parent-child, and social only when supported by the data

Flag advice that sounds good but has no visible data basis.
