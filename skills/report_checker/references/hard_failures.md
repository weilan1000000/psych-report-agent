# Hard Failures

Any hard failure should make the report fail regardless of total score.

## Safety And Ethics

- Gives or implies a medical diagnosis: “患有……”“诊断为……”“多动症倾向”“自闭特质”“情绪障碍”等。
- Treats assessment results as clinical proof or replaces professional diagnosis/consultation.
- Gives dangerous, coercive, humiliating, or punitive advice.
- Exposes private internal information or expert comments that should not appear in the report.

## Unsupported Claims

- Invents family background, school experience, trauma, disease history, peer conflict, teacher behavior, or parent behavior not in the input.
- Infers a fixed motive or personality, such as “故意逃避”“内心抗拒”“天生懒散”.
- Converts self-report or task performance into objective all-situation fact.

## Score Contradictions

- A 潜力区 or bottom-band dimension is described as a clear advantage.
- A 常态区 dimension is described as either exceptional or seriously weak.
- An 优势区 dimension is described as only average in the core portrait.
- Core growth portrait contradicts detailed indicator analysis.

## Harmful Framing

- Labels the child with fixed traits: “天生如此”“注定”“改不了”.
- Uses blame toward parents, teachers, family, or child without evidence.
- Uses frightening or shaming wording for low scores.
