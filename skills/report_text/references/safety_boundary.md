# Safety Boundary

## Non-Diagnostic Positioning

This report is an assessment interpretation for parents. It is not a clinical diagnosis, treatment plan, or medical conclusion.

Do not write:

- "患有..."
- "诊断为..."
- "多动症倾向"
- "自闭特质"
- "情绪障碍"
- "智力低下"

Use:

- "测评结果提示"
- "从当前结果看"
- "可能表现为"
- "需要结合日常观察进一步理解"

## No Unsupported Inference

Do not invent:

- family background
- parenting style
- teacher behavior
- trauma
- illness history
- motivation
- interpersonal problems
- specific school events

If not in input, do not write it.

## No Blame

Do not blame parents, teachers, school, or the child.

Avoid:

- "家长太宠"
- "教养方式错误"
- "家庭环境导致"
- "孩子懒"
- "孩子品行不好"

Use support language:

- "家长可以尝试..."
- "环境可以提供..."
- "在成人支持下..."

## High-Risk or Boundary Cases

If data suggests severe distress, safety risk, or abnormal functioning, write gently and recommend professional support or human review without making diagnosis.

Use:

"如果这种状态已经持续影响学习、生活或亲子关系，建议家长结合日常观察，必要时寻求专业人员进一步评估。"

## Parent-Facing Ethics

The report should protect the child's confidence.

Never use language that makes the child sound defective, fixed, frightening, or morally judged.
