# Secondary-School Growth Support And Planning

Use this reference only for an explicitly selected secondary-school report.

Open the section with one restrained principle: the suggestions grow from the
student's current profile, should be light enough to sustain, and become useful
through repeated use in real study and life.

This final section is the report's single home for action plans. Detailed
indicator blocks explain results and must not each carry their own exercise.
Combine several related indicators into three coherent suggestions so the
student receives a small number of meaningful actions rather than 26 repeated
instructions.

## Advice Formula

Build every suggestion from:

`data relationship -> real scene -> exact student action -> amount/time/tool -> adult role -> observable result -> review signal`

Each suggestion must cite two to four supplied indicators. Do not claim that
the student has already joined an activity, experienced a conflict, chosen a
major, or formed a stable preference unless the input says so.

The JSON field name remains `parent_suggestions` for backward compatibility,
but visible secondary-school advice is student-facing. Populate the fields as:

- `category`: use the three-category contract selected for the secondary stage
- `scene`: a real situation the student can recognize
- `action`: what the student can do independently
- `data_basis`: two to four supplied indicators
- `parent_wording`: a sentence the student can use to request support from a parent or teacher
- `first_step`: one action small enough to try this week

The `action` and `first_step` must use a concrete verb and leave visible
evidence. Prefer `写下三个方案并筛选一个` over `多训练创新思维`, and `本周完成一次
十分钟的小尝试并记录结果` over `加强实践`. The reader should not have to infer
what the first move is.

Every card must also state how the result will be reviewed: what evidence to
look at, and what would lead the student to retain, adjust, or replace the
method. A visible product without a review decision is still incomplete.

Do not write `parent_wording` as an adult command or praise script in a
secondary-school report.

## Junior-High Categories

Use exactly these three visible categories for junior-high reports:

1. `自我成长与学习规划`
2. `兴趣探索与项目实践`
3. `生涯启发与发展规划`

These categories replace the elementary labels `学习与日常`、`课外与探索` and
`亲子互动与沟通`. Do not merely rename an elementary parent card. Move action
ownership to the student and place parent/teacher help inside the support
sentence.

## Required Directions

### 1. Self-Growth And Study Planning

Let the student define the target, break down the task, act, and review. Adults
use coaching questions instead of monitoring every step.

- Junior high: use a light `weekly goal - daily task - one-sentence review`
  structure.
- Senior high: use `target subject - concrete bottleneck - key task - evidence
  of progress - weekly review`.

Prefer questions such as `这周最影响进度的是哪一类问题？` and
`你准备用什么证据判断自己有进步？` over commands or praise scripts.

For junior high, advice should sound doable rather than managerial. A useful
card normally contains one target, one tool, one review moment, and one support
request. Avoid elaborate tracking systems that are harder than the task itself.

### 2. Interest Exploration And Project Practice

Choose one small, real task related to the score profile. The student should
leave evidence of the process: a question, sources, alternatives, a decision,
revision notes, and a brief reflection.

For an open-ended problem, separate divergent and convergent thinking:

1. first generate several directions without early feasibility judgment;
2. then compare them with clear criteria and choose one for the next trial.

- Junior high: prioritize question notebooks, broad experiences, clubs,
  reading, experiments, and small inquiry projects.
- Senior high: prioritize subject-linked projects, research questions, data or
  product work, and records that help test sustained interest and problem-solving
  style.

The purpose is not a polished product. It is to observe whether the student is
willing to keep investing, enjoys the work process, and can transfer a method.

### 3. Career Inspiration And Development Planning

Treat a future direction as a hypothesis to test:

- connect it to two to four supplied indicators
- name no more than one or two broad direction clusters
- give one real validation activity, such as a course experience, club task,
  short project, interview, reading trail, or job-shadowing observation
- ask what evidence would show sustained interest, not momentary novelty
- state that later choices must also consider subject foundation, values,
  experience, and updated information

For junior high, prefer broad fields such as scientific inquiry, engineering
practice, information technology, design expression, communication, or public
service. Do not turn the card into a list of university majors or occupations.

## Adult Support Inside Every Category

State the role split clearly:

- the student chooses, acts, records, and reflects
- parents provide boundaries, time, information, and emotional steadiness
- teachers provide task nodes, role choices, criteria, and feedback

Do not write `尊重孩子` as a slogan. Show what adults stop doing, what structure
they retain, and what the student decides.

## Senior-High Extension

For senior high, retain the same three-category API contract but make the third
card more concrete about subject evidence, elective information, project
records, and major exploration. It remains a hypothesis, not a verdict.

Never write `适合某专业`, `应该报考`, or `未来会从事` based only on this
assessment. Avoid long menus of majors and occupations.

## Quality Check

Reject or revise advice when it:

- could be pasted into any student's report without changing the evidence
- contains only adult instructions and no student action
- has no observable review signal
- lists activities without explaining their relation to the profile
- treats AI literacy or career trends as generic slogans
- sounds like an elementary-school reward or parent-control plan
- speaks only to adults and never gives the student an owned action
- repeats `你可以` mechanically in every sentence instead of varying rhythm
