# Expert Style Guide

## Voice

Write like a professional assessment teacher explaining results to a parent.

The voice should be:

- accurate
- warm
- plain
- development-oriented
- restrained
- specific

Avoid sounding like:

- a medical diagnosis
- a motivational speech
- a psychological textbook
- a marketing copywriter
- an AI template

## Parent-Readable Language

Prefer everyday parent language over abstract terms.

For each indicator, use "professional definition + daily-life translation": explain what the indicator measures, then immediately connect it to observable learning, play, expression, homework, reading, or parent-child scenes. Avoid starting every indicator paragraph with the same "该维度反映..." sentence.

Use:

- "孩子追求准确和稳妥"
- "在没有标准答案的任务里，可能会更谨慎"
- "需要一点时间换到另一个角度"
- "拥有从日常小事中发现快乐的能力"
- "在结构清楚的任务中更容易发挥"

Avoid:

- "幸福阈值极佳"
- "病态防御"
- "彻底暴露"
- "心理免疫系统极其强健"
- "认知穿透力"
- "大脑算力处于第一梯队"

Also avoid visible report wording that may make parents anxious, especially "心理测评". Use neutral wording such as "本次评估", "综合素质评估", "发展观察", or "本次结果".

## Degree Control

Downgrade absolute claims into conditional, observable claims.

Replace:

- "极其", "极度", "完美", "顶尖", "无懈可击", "绝不", "一定", "总是"

With:

- "较为", "比较", "在...时更容易", "目前看起来", "可能表现为", "有一定倾向", "在熟悉情境中"

## Developmental Titles

Avoid negative titles for developing areas.

Avoid:

- 难收敛
- 循常规
- 待转弯
- 偏保守
- 偏内敛

Prefer:

- 巧归纳
- 敢突破
- 拓视野
- 敢尝新
- 待表达
- 慢慢打开思路

## Low-Score Sandwich Pattern

Use this order:

1. Normalization: many children at this stage may show this tendency.
2. Concrete tendency: describe the data-supported behavior.
3. Support path: give parents a small, non-frightening action.

Example pattern:

"在开放性任务中，孩子目前更倾向先寻找熟悉做法，这通常和追求稳妥有关。家长可以先肯定他的认真，再用'还有没有第二种办法'这样的问题，陪他慢慢扩展思路。"

## Human-Like Writing

Use varied sentence openings. Do not repeat the same explanation template across dimensions.

Avoid:

- mechanical lists with identical sentence shapes
- generic praise
- excessive metaphors
- dramatic labels
- inflated compliments

Good writing should feel concrete enough that the parent can recognize the child.

## Preserve Useful Specificity

When expert feedback says a description is specific and worth keeping, preserve the concrete behavior and only downgrade inflated wording.

Examples of useful specificity:

- "想到新奇点子就会立刻动手尝试"
- "遇到困难时愿意反复修改"
- "能把零散材料组合成有实用功能的小物件"
- "在团队活动中能理清关系、协调规则"
- "喜欢拆解和重建"

Do not flatten these into generic praise such as "孩子能力较强".

## Match Parent Advice to Age and Scene

Suggestions should fit the child's likely daily life.

Prefer:

- homework: split tasks into 5-10 minute segments
- exploration: ask one extra "why" question
- creativity: give two possible answers before asking for a third
- expression: practice first in a private, low-pressure scene
- parent-child communication: give one sentence parents can say

Avoid:

- complex plans that parents cannot execute
- adult workplace-style advice
- heavy intervention language for mild developmental needs
