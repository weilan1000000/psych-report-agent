# Common Errors From Pan and Tang Teacher Reviews

## 1. Internal Contradiction

The most serious repeated issue is contradiction between the core portrait and detailed dimensions.

Examples of contradiction types:

- Core portrait says the child depends on routine, but detailed innovation, curiosity, and thinking indicators are high.
- Core portrait says the child has shallow association, but originality and convergent thinking are high.
- A suggestion targets time management, while the real weak areas are curiosity or flexibility.

Required behavior:

- Reconcile mixed profiles instead of choosing a dramatic label.
- Explain coexistence: a child can be strong in logical reasoning while more cautious in open-ended creative tasks.
- If data conflicts, write cautiously and flag the conflict for review.

## 2. Labeling and Negative Titles

Avoid titles or descriptions that sound like fixed traits.

Problematic:

- "偏保守"
- "难收敛"
- "循常规"
- "极度依赖经验"
- "按部就班"
- "鸵鸟策略"

Better:

- "敢突破"
- "巧归纳"
- "需要拓展联想广度"
- "在开放任务中更追求稳妥"
- "需要更多时间权衡主线"

## 3. Absolute and Inflated Language

Pan teacher repeatedly corrected extreme wording.

Avoid:

- "极其", "极度", "极强", "无可争议", "一流", "顶尖", "无懈可击", "第一梯队", "定海神针"

Why:

- It creates unrealistic expectations for parents.
- It can pressure the child.
- It weakens scientific credibility.

Use conditional descriptions tied to data.

## 4. Academic or Aggressive Terms

Avoid language parents find hard to understand or emotionally harsh.

Problematic:

- "幸福阈值"
- "病态防御"
- "彻底暴露"
- "心理免疫系统"
- "认知穿透力"
- "神经加工速率犹如闪电"

Better:

- "能从日常小事中发现快乐"
- "会因为担心不够好而显得谨慎"
- "可以聊聊不完美时刻"
- "在学校生活中有较好的适应"

## 5. Self-Report Treated as Objective Fact

If a result comes from self-report, distinguish self-perception from objective ability.

Use:

- "从自评结果看"
- "孩子对自己的感受是"
- "这更接近孩子的主观体验"

Avoid:

- "孩子实际就是..."
- "说明孩子一定..."

## 6. Generic Advice

Suggestions fail when they are not linked to the child's actual profile.

Problematic:

- advice about orderliness when no data supports orderliness issues
- time-management suggestions when the actual developing area is thinking flexibility
- broad advice such as "多鼓励", "建立契约", "培养元认知"

Required:

- link each suggestion to a high or low dimension
- use strengths as a scaffold for developing areas
- include a parent sentence the parent can say

## 7. Repetition and Template Feel

Several comments ask for more individualized writing and less repeated wording.

Avoid copying the same dimension prose across children.

For each child, identify:

- the unique combination of high and low dimensions
- the tension between strengths and developing areas
- the most useful home support scene

## 8. Advice Intensity Does Not Match the Child

Some advice is directionally reasonable but too heavy, too complex, or not age-appropriate.

Problematic:

- using complicated intervention steps for a mild emotional strength or ordinary developmental feature
- asking parents to run a heavy plan when a simple home prompt would work
- giving advice that does not fit the child's age or daily life scene

Required:

- match the weight of advice to the data severity
- prefer a small action parents can try tonight
- avoid making normal development sound like a serious intervention target

## 9. High-Interest and Low-Question Profiles Need Transition Sentences

Some children may show high interest in activities but lower tendency to ask deep questions.

Do not write these as contradictions. Explain the distinction:

- interest curiosity: the child is attracted to new activities or materials
- question curiosity: the child actively asks why and explores deeper causes

Use:

"孩子对自己感兴趣的活动有较高投入，但在进一步追问原理或主动查证原因时，还需要成人用问题带一带。"

## 10. Distinguish Idea Quantity From Idea Originality

A child can produce fewer ideas but still have some original ideas.

Use:

"孩子产出的想法数量不一定很多，但其中不乏有个人特点的亮点。"

Avoid:

- saying the child is both extremely fluent and very blocked
- using a high-score template for all thinking dimensions

## 11. Avoid Trendy or Over-Stylized Language

Some descriptions are specific but too internet-like or dramatic.

Avoid:

- "拒绝内耗"
- "未来成就大事的必备素质"
- "天生效率大师"

Keep the concrete behavior and remove the performative style.

## 12. Score Band and Prose Strength Do Not Match

Tang teacher repeatedly corrected mismatches between a dimension's level and the prose.

Problematic:

- 潜力区写成“表现卓越”“优势突出”。
- 常态区写成“极好”“完美”，或写成明显缺陷。
- 优势区写成只是中等水平。
- 核心成长画像说孩子某方面突出，但详细指标显示该维度只是常态区。

Required:

- Write the level first in the data sketch.
- Let adjectives match the level.
- If a dimension is 常态区, use balanced language such as “有一定基础”“整体较稳定”“在多数情境中能够保持”。
- If a dimension is 潜力区, use developmental language such as “仍在发展”“需要更多支持”“可以通过具体练习逐步提升”。

## 13. Missing Preface and Reader Relationship

Tang teacher suggested adding a preface so parents and teachers feel respected and understand how to use the report.

Required:

- Open with a short respectful note.
- Explain the report is a reference for understanding and support.
- Avoid presenting the report as a final judgment about the child.

## 14. Suggestions Without Overall Logic

A repeated Tang comment: the original “互动指南” felt hard to follow because it lacked a whole-picture suggestion and did not show the purpose of each action.

Required:

- Start 成长支持建议 with an overall feedback paragraph.
- Then organize detailed advice by learning, thinking, social, and parent-child domains when relevant.
- For each advice item, make the data basis and purpose visible.

## 15. Confusing Indicator Names

“抑制控制(Nogo)” and “抑制控制(go)” are easily confused.

Required:

- Check the input definition before explaining either indicator.
- Do not infer the meaning from the name alone.
- If the input does not define the direction, write cautiously or flag it for review.
