# Core Growth Portrait Method

The core growth portrait is the report's interpretation thesis. It is not a
summary of every score and it is not a shorter version of the detailed metric
pages.

## Evidence Selection

Use only the indicators selected by `ReportPlannerAgent` in
`macro_plan.portrait_blueprint.selected_evidence`.

- Use 3-5 indicators in total.
- Prefer two related strengths from one or two domains over the first metrics in
  spreadsheet order.
- Use no more than two developing indicators.
- A normal-band indicator may provide balance, but must not be called a weakness.
- `evidence_used` must match the selected evidence and must never contain all 26
  metrics.

## Stage-Aware Argument

For elementary reports, write 260-420 Chinese characters in two or three short
paragraphs. For secondary-school reports, write 360-500 Chinese characters in
four short paragraphs so the first page carries a complete interpretation
rather than a brief encouragement.

1. Portrait thesis: name the child's current way of organizing strengths. Connect
   the strength domains in one sentence instead of opening with “整体来看”.
2. Strength mechanism and value: explain what those strengths may support when a
   task calls for them. Do not repeat levels or raw scores.
3. Support conditions: explain where the genuine support priority may matter and
   why it is worth practising, without turning it into a fixed weakness.
4. Development bridge: explain how one selected
   strength can become a practical starting point for it. Do not append a generic
   weakness caveat to an advantage.

Elementary reports may combine steps 3 and 4. Secondary-school reports should
keep them separate and end with a student-owned trial-and-review principle, not
a full suggestion card.

The portrait should answer: “What kind of development pattern do these results
form together?” It should not merely answer: “Which scores are high or low?”

## Fact Boundary

Assessment scores support tendencies, not biographies. Unless explicit background
or observations are supplied, do not assert:

- “孩子喜欢、经常、总是、习惯于、能够……”
- “在班里、小组活动中、学校里、与同学或老师……”
- a hobby, family event, school incident, learning history, or fixed personality

Use conditional translation instead:

- “当任务需要把想法转化为具体方案时，孩子更可能……”
- “在信息较多、需要持续比较的任务中，这项能力可能……”
- “如果把探索任务拆成可以动手验证的小步骤，已有的……优势可成为起点。”

Concrete scenes belong in suggestions as options for parents to try, not as facts
that supposedly already happened.

## Quality Check

Reject and rewrite the portrait when it:

- contains more than five evidence indicators;
- lists score bands without a profile thesis;
- separates strengths and support areas without explaining their relationship;
- invents observable history from scores;
- uses the same generic sentence for every child;
- calls a normal-band result a weakness or adds a weakness caveat to a clear
  advantage.
