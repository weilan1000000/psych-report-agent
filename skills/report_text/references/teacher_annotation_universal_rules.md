# Teacher Annotation Universal Rules

This reference records the six reusable writing corrections from the latest
teacher review. Apply it to both elementary and secondary reports. The
stage-specific skill still controls whether the visible reader is a parent or
the student.

## The Report Must Complete Four Jobs

The report as a whole should help the reader:

1. locate the examinee's current developmental position;
2. identify resources that can continue to be used;
3. identify directions that deserve practice without turning them into defects;
4. leave with one small action whose completion and effect can be observed.

The opening should state this purpose precisely. Do not use vague phrases such
as `先呈现整体画像` without explaining what the reader can learn or do with it.

Recommended purpose logic:

`定位现阶段 -> 认识可用资源 -> 看见可发展方向 -> 选择下一项可验证行动`

## Remove Mechanical AI Hinges

Do not use the following as routine paragraph hinges:

- `不是……而是……`
- `这提示……`
- `这说明……`
- `按输入的量表解释……`
- `一个可操作的起点是……`
- repeated `整体来看`、`综合来看`、`本次结果显示`

These phrases may appear only when their logical contrast or inference is
indispensable and the sentence cannot be stated more directly. Never repeat
them as a template across indicators.

Write the conclusion directly:

- Avoid: `按输入的量表解释，本项处于常态区。`
- Use: `本项指标目前处于常态区。`
- Avoid: `这提示你需要继续练习。`
- Use: `在开放任务中，先写下三个不同方向，再比较哪一种更值得推进。`

## Make Developmental Language Readable

Keep the professional construct accurate, then translate it into a task the
selected reader can picture.

Use this order for indicator interpretation:

`professional construct -> verified level -> task scene -> likely performance -> developmental meaning or influencing condition`

- Keep one main idea in each sentence.
- Explain unavoidable jargon in the same sentence or the next short sentence.
- Prefer study, discussion, inquiry, project, collaboration, self-management,
  and emotional-recovery scenes over abstract developmental theory.
- Do not write like a textbook, academic paper, or diagnostic record.
- Do not expose brain-region or neural-mechanism claims merely to sound
  professional. Replace phrases such as `大脑的高级指挥官`、`神经信息加工速率`
  and `底层逻辑的内驱力` with the concrete cognitive task the metric actually
  measures. Keep the construct accurate without making a causal brain claim
  that the score cannot establish.
- Do not invent a past event. Use conditional wording such as `在……任务中`,
  `当需要……时`, and `面对……时`.
- Keep indicator analysis focused on interpretation. Do not attach a training
  step, time requirement, tool, artifact, or review rule to every metric.

## Suggestions Must Be Operational

A suggestion is complete only when it contains:

`trigger scene + exact action + amount/time/tool + visible result + review rule`

The reader should know what to do today and how to tell whether it helped.

- Weak: `多进行发散思维练习。`
- Strong: `遇到开放题时，先用三分钟写下三个不同方向，不急着判断可行性；再选出其中两个，按时间、资源和效果进行比较，留下一个准备继续验证的方案。`

Use concrete verbs such as:

`写下、列出、圈出、比较、选择、完成、制作、记录、查找、验证、复盘`

Use observable units where useful:

`一个问题、两条资料、三个方案、十分钟、一周、一次小作品、一张清单`

Apply this rule to the final support suggestions. Together, those cards should
let the student answer all four questions:

1. What exactly do I do?
2. How much or how long do I do it?
3. What do I leave behind: a card, table, recording, sketch, score, or result?
4. What evidence tells me to retain, adjust, or replace the method?

Do not distribute these action protocols across every indicator block. That
creates repetition and weakens the distinction between interpretation and
support planning. The completed elementary indicator-analysis format also
remains unchanged.

Band-specific indicator interpretation:

- Advantage: explain stable availability and developmental value. Do not
  append an automatic weakness caveat.
- Normal: explain the usable foundation and the conditions that may affect
  stability.
- Potential: explain the current manifestation, why the ability matters, and
  the task conditions under which fluctuation is more likely. Do not use a
  deficit label.

## Divergent And Convergent Thinking Method

When supporting open-ended problem solving, use two explicit stages:

1. Quantity stage: temporarily suspend early feasibility judgment and generate
   several different directions.
2. Selection stage: compare the directions with clear criteria, retain the
   stronger options, and choose one for the next trial.

Do not compress both stages into `多想几个办法` or `提高创新能力`.

## Stage Ownership

- Elementary: explain the child to the parent, then give the parent a concrete
  support action and an observable child response.
- Secondary: speak directly to the student. The student chooses, acts, records,
  and reviews; adults provide time, information, boundaries, or feedback.

The common method stays the same. Only voice, scene maturity, and action
ownership change.

## Final Self-Check

Reject or revise the draft when:

- the opening does not explain the report's practical purpose;
- a paragraph relies on AI-style contrast or inference hinges;
- developmental language is too academic for the selected reader;
- a final support suggestion is only a concept and has no first action;
- a final suggested action has no observable result or review signal;
- an indicator result-analysis paragraph contains an exercise or action plan;
- two indicators reuse the same result-analysis paragraph;
- the text contains internal pipeline language such as `按输入的量表解释`.
