# Secondary-School Writing Style

Use this reference only when the deterministic stage router selects
`secondary`, `junior_high`, or `senior_high`.

## Reader And Subject

Write the visible report directly for the student. Use `你` as the normal
subject and let the student set goals, make choices, act, record, and reflect.
Parents and teachers are secondary readers who provide boundaries,
information, structure, resources, feedback, and emotional steadiness; they do
not replace the student's decisions.

The stage overlay changes voice and action ownership, not evidence standards.
Keep all common project rules for data faithfulness, professional definitions,
score-band calibration, non-diagnostic safety, and no invented experience.

Use calm, natural spoken Chinese suitable for a 13-15-year-old. It should sound
like a thoughtful teacher or counselor speaking with the student, not like a
parent lecturing, a textbook definition, or internet slang. Prefer terms such as:

- 当前可用的发展资源
- 可迁移的学习方法
- 值得温和关注的成长空间
- 目标、证据、选择、行动、复盘

Avoid elementary-school phrasing based on rewards, games, adult arrangement,
or constant parent supervision. Avoid academic jargon, fixed-trait labels,
diagnosis, and deterministic education or career claims.

Avoid visible phrases such as `孩子目前……`, `家长可以……`, `请家长留意……`,
`家长/老师您好`. Prefer `你目前更容易……`, `你可以先试……`, `如果需要支持，
可以和家长或老师商量……`.

## Opening And Rhythm

- Greet the student by name when a safe display name is supplied: `X同学，你好：`.
- Explain the report purpose in two short sentences: help the student locate
  the current stage, recognize usable resources, identify directions worth
  developing, and choose one small action to test. Do not use a ceremonial
  parent letter, excessive gratitude, or the vague phrase `先呈现整体画像`
  without explaining its practical value.
- Keep most sentences between 15 and 35 Chinese characters. Mix short and
  medium sentences; avoid four consecutive sentences with the same opening.
- Use concrete school-life contexts such as planning homework, understanding a
  new rule, discussing a project, asking a question, handling feedback, and
  restoring attention. Write them as conditional situations, not invented past
  events.

## Core Portrait

Use a concise profile headline built from a contrast:

`较突出资源 + 可继续发展的方向`

Examples of structure, not reusable conclusions:

- `认知潜能资源较突出，创新行为仍可继续发展`
- `兴趣效能资源较突出，开放任务能力仍可继续提升`

Write 360-500 Chinese characters as four short paragraphs:

1. State the main cross-module pattern and connect the selected strength clusters.
2. Explain the shared strength mechanism, its value, and how it may transfer
   into learning, collaboration, or project work.
3. Explain up to three genuine support indicators and the task conditions in
   which they matter, without turning them into fixed weaknesses.
4. Show how one existing strength may become a bridge, then end with one
   student-owned trial-and-review principle without expanding into an advice card.

An advantage is not only a high result. Explain how the student may learn to
name the method, reuse it, and test it in another subject or project. A support
priority is not a fixed weakness. Explain what clearer structure, feedback, or
practice may help the student do next.

## Indicator Analysis

Keep the same evidence order used by the project:

`professional definition -> verified level -> direct student-facing task meaning -> developmental value or influencing condition`

The task meaning should describe likely performance in study, collaboration,
open tasks, inquiry, or self-management. Use conditional wording when the input
does not contain a real event. Do not turn every block into an instruction to
parents.

Preserve the construct meaning of the local professional definition, but make
the person reference age-appropriate: `儿童/孩子/个体` may become `你` when this
does not change the construct. The result-analysis sentences should normally
use `你`, not `孩子`.

- Advantage: describe stable availability and transfer value. Do not append a
  routine weakening caveat.
- Normal: describe the usable foundation and the task conditions that may make
  performance vary.
- Potential: describe the current support priority and the structure or
  task conditions that may affect stability; do not write a deficit label.

The indicator page is interpretive, not prescriptive. Do not include a weekly
exercise, exact action, time or quantity requirement, tool, visible artifact,
or review rule in each metric block. Concentrate those elements in the final
three support suggestions, where the selected indicators can be integrated
into a smaller number of coherent actions.

Vary sentence openings and rhythm. Preserve the writing logic, not repeated
phrases such as `本次结果显示`, `整体来看`, or `建议从……开始`.
Also avoid routine AI hinges such as `不是……而是……` and `这提示……`.
State the task meaning directly. The fixed level sentence should
use `本项指标目前处于……`, never `按输入的量表解释……`.

## Junior-High Emphasis

Prioritize autonomy awakening, habit formation, task decomposition, question
formation, and broad experience. Career content must remain directional
exploration through courses, clubs, reading, projects, and real tasks.

For a junior-high report, avoid premature major selection and long-term career
claims. The most useful next step is usually a small method the student can test
for one week and review with evidence.

## Senior-High Emphasis

Prioritize target subjects, bottleneck diagnosis, evidence of weekly progress,
project records, elective information, and major exploration. Present a study
or career direction as a hypothesis to test, never as a verdict.
